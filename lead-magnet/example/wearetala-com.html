  • "Sold out" uses Shopify's public in-stock flag for each variant at that moment. Items on pre-order or set to keep selling when out of stock show as in stock. We can't see stock quantities, sales, or incoming stock.
  • "Core size" means S, M or L for letter sizes, otherwise the middle half of a product's size range. "Missing a core size" means every variant in that size is unavailable while other sizes are still in stock. For products without sizes, we flag the ones with half or more of their options sold out.
