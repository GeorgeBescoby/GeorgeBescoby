### Round 1 fixes
1. **Truth:** removed "so last year's Black Friday is there to compare against". It now says "Connecting your store pulls in your historical data too", which is exactly the claim in the brief.
2. **Bridge:** rewrote the call to action. "This map stops where your own data starts. Every number above uses a guessed margin and no ad data." Then a concrete question in their terms: "did the Atomiser offer pay for its ads yesterday?" It's generated from the live offer product, not hardcoded.
3. **Margin fairness (⚠️ in truth):** the findings subtitle now says "If yours is higher, read the 60% or 70% column in the table below and the numbers get easier." The live offer card already shows all four margins (never / 15.3× / 4.9× / 2.9×).
4. **Style:** the rewrite briefly said "during the sale, not after it", which breaks the no "not X but Y" rule. Changed to "while the sale is running". Checked again: no em-dashes, no "not X but Y".
5. **Phone layout:** tables showed one margin column before scrolling. Tightened them so all four margin columns fit at 390px with no horizontal page scroll.
