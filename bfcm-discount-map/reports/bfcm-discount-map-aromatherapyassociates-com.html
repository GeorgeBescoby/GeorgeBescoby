What this can't tell you

  • Your real margin. We can see prices and VAT. We can't see what each product costs you to make, so every number is shown across a range of margins.
  • Your live ROAS per product during the sale. The map says what each discount needs. Only your ad and order data can say what each product is getting, hour by hour, while the sale runs.
  • Whether the first-order offer pays back. A first order that loses money can still be worth it if the customer comes back. That depends on your repeat rate, which only your store data shows.

Assumptions and sources

  • List price is the full price in your product feed. Where a product is marked down today, the compare-at price is used.
  • VAT at full price is read from your checkout's "Including £X in taxes" line (checkout). Where checkout showed a sale price, the VAT is scaled up to full price.
  • Sale price = list price × (1 − discount). Net revenue = sale price − VAT × (1 − discount).
  • Product cost = (list price − VAT) × (1 − margin). It stays the same whatever the discount.
  • Payment fees = 1.9% of the sale price + £0.25.
  • Postage per order: £2.50 letterbox, £3.50 small boxed, £5 gift sets and bundles, £15 large items, £4 otherwise. Classes are our guess from the product name and type.
  • Contribution = net revenue − product cost − fees − postage. Break-even ROAS = sale price ÷ contribution. Breaking point = the discount where contribution is £0. Anything above 50× is shown as 50×+.
  • One unit per order, no free delivery threshold, no returns. Free gifts with purchase are left out, which flatters the numbers.
  • Featured products: your Black Friday collection first, newest first, in stock, one per product type, plus the product in your live offer. Gift cards, samples, spare parts, hidden products and anything under £3 are left out.
  • Last year: 25% off sitewide with code BF25. The Black Friday collection page is titled "Black Friday Sale 2025" and says "Don't miss 25% off sitewide*, with code BF25 at checkout ... offer ends 7th of December 2025. *Exclusions apply."
