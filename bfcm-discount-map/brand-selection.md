## Pick: Aromatherapy Associates

It has the strongest Black Friday anchor on the list, on both counts:

- **Last year, dated and specific.** Their own Black Friday collection page still names 2025, the % off (25% sitewide), the code (BF25) and the end date (7 December 2025).
- **A live offer this year with a clear % off.** The Atomiser diffuser is £78 against £130 (40% off), promoted on the homepage. 19 products in the Black Friday collection carry live markdowns (14 in stock). A free Rose Gift worth £38 comes with orders over £75.
- **Clean data.** 123 products in the feed and 114 after filters. Every featured product returned a VAT line at checkout. It's a physical product brand with a wide price range (£15 to £130), which makes the breaking points vary.

WUKA came second. Its 2025 page is the most detailed, but nothing meaningful is live now. Skinician has a live offer, but its Black Friday page has no year, so I can't label it "last year" with confidence.

## Things to know about the pick

- The site footer says "Proud to be part of The Boots Group". The Shopify store is mid-sized by Storeleads' estimate, but the parent is large. Check the contact owns their own paid media before sending.
- The live discounts sit under "Last Chance" / "The Seasonal Edit". The site doesn't call them Black Friday. The report and emails call them a live offer ahead of Black Friday, not a Black Friday offer.
